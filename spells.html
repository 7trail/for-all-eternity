Homebrew Spells

Astral Echo

Level: 2 School: Evocation

Casting Time: 1 bonus action

Range: 60 feet

Components: V, S

Duration: Instantaneous (echo lasts until the start of your next turn)

Your magic ripples through space, forming a small echo at point within range. This echo glows with dim light for 10 feet. As long as this echo is present, the next spell you cast will be cast as though it was cast from that position. The echo dissipates at the start of your next turn.

Bubbling Brew

Level: 0 (Cantrip) School: Transmutation

Casting Time: 1 action

Range: 15 feet

Components: V, S, M (a glass bottle filled with any liquid)

Duration: 1 minute

You conjure a small, fizzing bottle of thick, iridescent liquid that bubbles with potential. Throwing the bottle causes it to splash onto a creature within 15 feet, consuming both the bottle and granting them one effect of your choice for the duration:

  • They gain advantage on their next ability check
  • They gain a +1 bonus to AC
  • They gain 1d6 temporary hit points
  • They gain the ability to speak one language you know

Verdant Embrace

Level: 1 School: Transmutation

Casting Time: 1 action

Range: 30 feet

Components: V, S, M (a sprig of ivy)

Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Thick vines come up through the ground at a point you see within range. A creature within 5 feet of these vines must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained. While restrained, the vines entangle them and bloom with luminescent flowers, blooming with your favorite color.

As a bonus action on each of your turns, these blooms emit healing spores, healing the restrained creature for hit points equal to 1d6 plus your proficiency bonus. The spell ends if the target is freed.

At Higher Levels: When cast using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, increase health restored by this spell by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1st.
