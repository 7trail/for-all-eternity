History

In the years following the cataclysmic events on the mainland, a number of residents left to start a new life. Among them was the now-retired Emeritus Jonathan Quidd, known for his role in quelling the Inferno. (Lesser known is his role in causing the Inferno.) Including him, a number of brilliant minds formed a strong economic backbone on Lunar Island, bringing more and more people to the booming villages.

Evil was brewing underneath. Operating out of a secluded location on the mainland, a madman was working fervently to unknown ends. Unfortunately, his potential was limited, forcing him to relocate to a more suitable location.

In an instant, a mountain was levelled. He had used up all the magic he could muster, moving his secluded castle to the center of Lunar Island, where there would again be enough 'material' for his experiments.

A crimson wall (known in writings as the Blood Veil) was formed around Lunar Island, blocking all interference entering- or exiting- the island. This effectively trapped all people within the confines of the Blood Veil.

Everybody knows they are simply waiting to become victims. For fear of being found by him, most people stay underground, where it's safer.

What is he up to? How long will you be kept here?

These are questions you have been asking for two decades.

There is, of course, only one surefire way to find out.

Pantheon

Veira, the Sheltering Light

Goddess of Family and Health. She is known to bring bountiful harvests to those who hide underground.

Athena of the Cosmos

Goddess of the Stars and Fate. She is said to guide those who surface to their destinations.

Yukionna, the Shattered

Goddess of Love and the Seasons. Her power is limited, but she protects those she can.

???

There are rumors of a fourth god, still mortal, working to rid Lunar Island of its captor. She has not been spotted in many years.

The 'False Gods'

Emeritus Jonathan Quidd

While not technically a god, he is seen as a deific figure due to the ubiquity of his work across the island. Not all of it has been found.

Amalia the Spirited
